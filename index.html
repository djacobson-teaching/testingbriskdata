More tools, more use

Typical all-time actions, by number of tools used

For PD, the goal is repeated use on real work, across more than one tool. Teachers who get past about 100 uses, or use four or more tools, tend to keep going. This data shows the pattern. It can't prove which comes first.

Next moves

Three groups where support could matter most.

Each group needs a different kind of nudge.

8

Former regulars

These teachers had 100+ actions in past years and haven't used Brisk this year.

Try: a quick personal check-in. Ask what changed.
14

Create-only users

Active this year, but they have only used Create. That's 29% of this year's users.

Try: run Give Feedback or Inspect Writing on student work they already have.
24

Not yet started

These teachers haven't tried Brisk. Only 4 new users have joined this year.

Try: pair each with a regular user for one real task, start to finish.

About this data

  • Source: Brisk usage export for 115 teachers, with this school year to date and all-time totals. "Past years" means all-time minus this year.
  • An action is one use of a Brisk tool. Counts show how often teachers use Brisk, not the quality of the work.
  • Teacher names are hidden. Every chart shows patterns, not people.
  • This year's shares move with a few heavy users. One teacher accounts for 830 of this year's 1,991 Boost actions.
